# 15 — 30 / 60 / 90-Day Optimisation Plan

## Pre-launch (week 0): gates
Credentials verified (doc 01), legal review of privacy/terms, fees decided, GHL + tracking verified (doc 13), founder photo, 3–6 real project photos if any exist.

## Days 1–30: Launch & learn (signal quality > volume)
**Goals:** working measurement end-to-end; first 20–40 leads; first discovery calls; learn lead quality by funnel.
- Launch Google G1, G4, G5, G7 and Meta A1, B1, plus retargeting R2.
- Search term review 3×/week; add negatives.
- Weekly lead-quality review: leads by tier × source × creative; call notes tagged with reasons (fit / not fit).
- Validate scoring against reality: for every discovery call, sales records "true fit: Y/N". Compare to tier.
- Speed-to-lead: priority leads contacted < 1 business day, every time.
- Publish articles #4 and #8.
- **KPIs to establish baselines (no targets yet):** CPL, % priority+standard, lead→booked, booked→show, show→qualified, qualified→paid assessment.

## Days 31–60: Tighten & qualify
**Goals:** cost per *qualified* lead falling; first paid assessments.
- Recalibrate `lead-scoring.json` using 30-day fit data (e.g. if "planning" stage converts, raise its weight). Ship via `LEAD_SCORING_JSON`.
- Turn on Google Ads offline imports (Qualified, Paid Assessment). Move G1/G5 to tCPA on Lead once ≥ 30 conv/30d.
- Meta: kill bottom 50% creatives by cost-per-qualified. Produce 3 new hooks from real sales-call objections. Launch founder video.
- Landing-page A/B test #1 (one variable): `/lp/new-home` headline A (current) vs B ("Verify your land and budget before you build in Ghana"). Split via Vercel Edge Config/middleware or ad-level URLs (`/lp/new-home?v=b`) with a `variant` param. Decide on qualified-lead rate, not CPL, with ≥ 100 visitors/variant minimum and treat results as directional at this volume.
- Add form-step abandonment analysis: if step 3 (budget) drop-off > 35%, test moving budget to step 4 or adding "Not sure yet" emphasis.
- Publish articles #10, #5 (with reviewer), #19.
- Collect first case-study permissions from clients in progress.

## Days 61–90: Scale what's proven
**Goals:** predictable cost per paid assessment; second channel scaled.
- Switch Google primary goal to Qualified Lead (or Paid Assessment value-based if ≥ 15–20/month).
- Meta: move optimisation to Schedule (or a CRM-based conversion-leads optimisation, if eligible) once volume supports it.
- Scale winning campaign budgets in 20% steps every 3–4 days while cost-per-qualified holds.
- Launch Funnel C (investors) broader if A/B are profitable. Test Canada as a separate campaign.
- Evaluate intent-data pilot (doc 16) only if first-party funnel economics are known.
- Publish first verified case study (if documented + permitted). Add real project photos to LPs and creative.
- Quarterly review: scoring rules, consent text, privacy policy, structured data, page speed.

## Weekly reporting template (sheet columns)
`Week · Channel · Campaign · Spend · Clicks · LP Views · form_start · Leads · Priority · Standard · Nurture · Booked · Showed · Qualified · Paid Assessments · Contracts · CPL · Cost/Qualified · Cost/Paid Assessment · Notes`
Sources: Ads platforms (spend), GA4 (funnel events), GHL (tiers & stages by utm_campaign/utm_content).
